Due Diligence: Verifying Financial Institutions

18 Jun 202612 min readGlobalSafe Research Team

Every year, billions of dollars are lost to fraudulent financial platforms. This guide provides a systematic framework for verifying institutional legitimacy before committing capital.

1. Licensing Verification

Every legitimate financial institution must hold a valid license from a recognized regulatory authority. In Kazakhstan, this means ARDFM (Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market) and the National Bank.

If an institution cannot provide a license number or is absent from the official registry, it is definitively fraudulent.

Verify through the official ARDFM registry or call the hotline at 1434.

2. Domain Analysis

Fraudulent platforms often create websites that closely mimic legitimate brokers. Key indicators:

  • Recently registered domain (check via WHOIS)
  • Domain name differs by 1-2 characters from legitimate platforms
  • No company information in footer or legal pages
  • Contact information limited to messaging apps only

3. Red Flags

Immediately cease engagement if any of the following are observed:

  • Guaranteed returns of 10-50% per month
  • Requests to transfer funds to personal accounts
  • Withdrawal blocks requiring "tax" or "commission" payments
  • Artificial urgency: "now or never" pressure tactics
  • Fabricated profit screenshots

4. Recovery Protocol

If funds have already been transferred:

  • Do not panic — you have a 72-hour critical window
  • Document all communications, receipts, and transaction records
  • Contact your bank immediately to initiate chargeback proceedings
  • File reports with law enforcement (102) and ARDFM (1434)
  • Engage legal counsel for professional assessment
